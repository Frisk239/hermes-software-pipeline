# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Hermes Software Pipeline a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive, evidence-based feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and community;
- respecting security, privacy, licensing, and attribution boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome attention;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring contributors to disclose credentials, private source, identity data, or sensitive evidence;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to Repository Governance Owner `Frisk239` through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. The owner will review reports fairly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, or participation that violate this policy.

Enforcement actions should be proportionate to the impact and pattern of behavior and may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces. Anyone directly involved in a report must recuse from deciding it when an alternative maintainer is available.
